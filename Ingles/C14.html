PHRASAL VERBS

¿Qué son los Phrasal Verbs?

02 / 46

Un phrasal verb es una combinación de un verbo + una partícula (preposición o adverbio) que crea un nuevo significado, diferente al verbo original.

📝 Ejemplo básico
look = mirar
look for = buscar
"I'm looking for my keys."
(Estoy buscando mis llaves.)
📝 Otro ejemplo
give = dar
give up = rendirse
"Don't give up on your dreams!"
(¡No te rindas en tus sueños!)
📌
Importante: El significado del phrasal verb a veces es imposible de adivinar por las palabras individuales. ¡Es mejor memorizarlos!
PHRASAL VERBS

Tipos de Phrasal Verbs

03 / 46
📝 Separables

El objeto puede ir entre el verbo y la partícula.

Turn the lights off.
Turn off the lights.
Apaga las luces.

Con pronombres (me, you, him, her, it, us, them):
Turn them off. ✅
Turn off them. ❌
📝 Inseparables

El objeto siempre va después de la partícula.

Look for your keys.
Look for them.
Busca tus llaves.

❌ Look your keys for.
❌ Look them for.

GRAMÁTICA

AS...AS – Comparaciones de igualdad

33 / 46

AS...AS se usa para comparar dos cosas que son iguales en algún aspecto.

As + adjetivo/adverbio + as
✅ She is as tall as her sister.
Ella es tan alta como su hermana.

✅ He runs as fast as me.
Él corre tan rápido como yo.

✅ This book is as interesting as that one.
Este libro es tan interesante como ese.
📌
El segundo AS es obligatorio. ¡No lo olvides!
GRAMÁTICA

NOT AS...AS – Desigualdad

34 / 46

NOT AS...AS se usa para comparar dos cosas que NO son iguales en algún aspecto.

Not + as + adjetivo/adverbio + as
✅ She is not as tall as her sister.
Ella no es tan alta como su hermana.

✅ He doesn't run as fast as me.
Él no corre tan rápido como yo.

✅ This book is not as interesting as that one.
Este libro no es tan interesante como ese.

GRAMÁTICA

AS...AS – Variaciones

36 / 46
📝 AS MUCH AS

Con sustantivos incontables (cantidad).

I don't have as much money as you.
No tengo tanto dinero como tú.

She doesn't drink as much coffee as me.
Ella no toma tanto café como yo.
📝 AS MANY AS

Con sustantivos contables (número).

I have as many books as you.
Tengo tantos libros como tú.

She has as many friends as her sister.
Ella tiene tantos amigos como su hermana.
💡 Ejemplo combinado:
I have as many shoes as she does, but not as much money as she does.
GRAMÁTICA

AS...AS – Errores comunes

37 / 46
❌ Errores comunes
❌ She is as taller as me.
✅ She is as tall as me.
(No uses comparativos con AS...AS.)

❌ He runs so fast as me.
✅ He runs as fast as me.
(Usa AS, no SO.)

❌ This book is not as interesting that one.
✅ This book is not as interesting as that one.
(No olvides el segundo AS.)
✅ Recordatorio
AS + adjetivo BASE + AS
¡No uses comparativos (taller, faster)!
¡No olvides el segundo "as"!
Usa AS MUCH AS (incontable) y AS MANY AS (contable).

AS...AS – Resumen

43 / 46
⚖️ AS...AS
  • Función: Comparar dos cosas iguales
  • Estructura: as + adjetivo/adverbio + as
  • Negativo: not as...as (no tan...como)
  • AS MUCH AS: con sustantivos incontables
  • AS MANY AS: con sustantivos contables
She is as tall as her sister.
I am not as tall as my father.
I have as many books as you.
I don't have as much money as you.
